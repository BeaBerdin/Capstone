# PATHWISE User Guide

Short step-by-step instructions for the main tasks of each role. Menu names
are the ones in the left sidebar; button names are shown in **bold**.

Every role can open **Settings** (bottom of the sidebar) to change the
profile photo, password, appearance, two-factor authentication and passkeys,
or to **Deactivate account**.

---

## Student

### Create an account

1. On the home page, choose **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email and password, choose **Student**, then
   **Create account**.
3. Open the email from PATHWISE and click the verification link. If it did
   not arrive, use **Resend verification email**.

### Enroll in a course

1. Open **Marketplace** and choose **View Course** on a course.
2. Free course: click **Enroll for Free**.
3. Paid course: click **Purchase Course**. You are taken to PayMongo; pay
   with one of the methods shown (for example GCash, Maya or Card). The
   enrollment starts as soon as PayMongo confirms the payment.
4. Your payments are listed under **Transactions**. If a transaction asks
   for a receipt, open it, choose the payment method, upload the receipt and
   click **Submit Payment Proof**. The Super Admin reviews it.

### Study a course

1. Open **My Courses** and click **Continue Learning**.
2. Open a lesson, read or watch it, then click **Mark as Complete**.
3. For a quiz, click **Take Quiz**, answer the questions and click
   **Submit Quiz**. If the quiz has a time limit, it is shown at the top.
   You can use **Retake Quiz** if you want a better score.

### Submit an assignment

1. Open **Assignments** and choose the assignment.
2. Type your answer or upload a file (the page shows which formats are
   accepted), then click **Submit Assignment**.
3. Your score and the teacher's feedback appear on the same page after
   grading.

### Get your certificate

1. When you finish a course, the certificate is issued automatically.
2. Open **Certificates**, click **View Certificate**, then **Download PDF**.

### Learning paths and recommendations

- **Learning Paths**: click **Generate AI Learning Path** for a suggested
  order of courses.
- **AI Recommendations**: courses suggested for you; click **View Course**.

## Teacher

### Create a course

1. Register and choose **Teacher**, then verify your email.
2. Open **My Courses** and click **Create Course**.
3. Fill in the title, description, category, price and cover image. You can
   **Start from a template** to get ready-made lesson titles. Click
   **Create Course**; it is saved as a draft.

### Add lessons and quizzes

1. In **My Courses**, open the course menu and choose **Manage Lessons**.
2. Click **Add Lesson**, choose the type (video, document, text or quiz),
   fill it in and click **Save Lesson**. Tick **Publish lesson** so students
   can see it.
3. For a quiz lesson, click **Open Quiz Builder**:
   - add questions one by one with **Add Question**, or
   - use **Generate with Gemini** to create questions from your lessons,
     then check them;
   - click **Save Quiz Settings** and **Publish quiz**.

### Submit the course for approval

1. In **My Courses**, choose **Submit for Approval** on the course.
2. The course stays locked while the administrator reviews it.
3. If it is **returned for revision**, read the administrator's feedback,
   make the changes and submit it again. When it is approved, it is
   published in the Marketplace.

### Create assignments and grade submissions

1. Open **Assignments** and click **New Assignment**. Choose the course,
   the type (for example essay or code) and the due date, then
   **Create Assignment**.
2. To grade, click **View Submissions**, then **Review Submission**.
3. Optional: click **Generate AI Evaluation** for a suggested score and
   feedback. Students never see it until you grade. You can click
   **Use AI Score** or enter your own score.
4. Click **Save Final Grade**, or **Return for Revision** to ask the student
   to resubmit.
5. The **Dashboard** lists AI evaluations still waiting for your review.

### Invite students and follow their progress

- **Student Invitations**: click **Create Invitation** for a course, then
  choose a registered student and click **Send Invitation**. Each
  invitation can be used once.
- **Student Progress** and **Quiz Results**: see how each student is doing.
- **Performance Analytics**: charts for your courses.
- To remove a student from a course: **My Courses** → **View Students** →
  **Request Removal**. An administrator decides on the request.

## Administrator (Department Head)

### Approve or return a course

1. Open **Courses** and use the status filter to show **Pending** courses.
2. Open the course to check its lessons and quizzes.
3. Click **Approve** to publish it, or **Return for Revision** and write
   feedback for the teacher (feedback is required).

### Manage enrollments

1. Open **Enrollments**.
2. Use **Edit** on an enrollment to change its status. Setting it to
   cancelled blocks the student's access but keeps their progress; setting
   it back to active restores access.

### Student removal requests

1. Open **Removal Requests**.
2. Read the teacher's reason, then **Approve** (the student is removed from
   the course) or **Reject**.

### Certificates

- **Certificate Template**: set the title, text and signatory printed on
  certificates, then **Save Template**.
- **Certificate Records**: issue a certificate with **Add Certificate**, or
  use **Edit** to change or revoke one.

### Monitor learning

- **Student Progress**, **Quiz Results**, **AI Recommendations** and
  **Learning Paths** show how students are doing across the courses.

## Super Admin (EDP)

### Manage user accounts

1. Open **User Management**.
2. Click **Add User**, choose the role (Administrator, Teacher, Student or
   Super Admin), fill in the details and click **Create Account**.
3. To remove someone's access, click **Deactivate**. Use **Activate** to
   give access back. Accounts are never deleted, so their records are kept.
4. **Mark verified** marks an account's email address as verified.

### Departments

1. Open **Department Management** and click **Create Department**.
2. Use **Assign Department** to put a user in a department.

### Verify payments

1. Open **Transactions**. The **Payment Verification Queue** lists receipts
   waiting for review.
2. Click **View** to open the student's receipt.
3. Click **Approve** to enroll the student, or **Reject** if the payment
   cannot be confirmed.
4. PayMongo payments are confirmed automatically and need no action.

### Reports

1. Open **Reports** to see enrollments, revenue, assignment activity and
   user engagement.
2. Click **Export PDF** to download the report.
